# Industry Intelligence Specification

## Objective
Define sector and industry analysis standards that power peer context, structure analysis, and competitive positioning.

## Business Rules
- Industry definitions must be versioned and source-referenced.
- Peer groups must be explicit and reviewable.
- Structural shifts must be tracked as time-bound events.
- Industry factors must be linked to valuation drivers.

## Functional Requirements
- Define sector/industry taxonomy governance.
- Define peer group construction rules.
- Define market-share and structure signal requirements.
- Define competitive force scoring requirements.
- Define industry-level catalyst and risk framework.

## Non-Functional Requirements
- Refresh frequency targets for industry datasets.
- Consistency requirements across industry snapshots.
- Explainability requirements for peer/industry scores.
- Scalability requirements for multi-sector coverage expansion.
- Data-quality thresholds for industry comparables.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Taxonomy policy is documented and approved.
- Peer selection criteria are documented.
- Industry scoring rubric is documented.
- Industry-to-valuation linkage is documented.
- Validation plan for industry metrics is approved.

## Edge Cases
- Conglomerates spanning multiple industries.
- Rapidly evolving sectors with unstable taxonomy fit.
- Sparse disclosure in niche industries.
- Temporary demand shocks distorting peer comparability.

## Out of Scope
- Proprietary sell-side sector narratives.
- Macroeconomic forecasting engine internals.
- Country allocation strategy.
